Fried Egg

Description

First, breaks the egg and the mix it in a bowl after adding a little salt before you heat your oil in the frying pan on the stove and then pour the egg mixture into the fryting pan and let the egg fry.

Ingredients

  • Egg
  • Oil
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Break egg into a bowl
  2. Add little salt and stir with spoon
  3. Pour into heatend frying pan with oil
  4. Wait till its fried and serve
